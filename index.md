# Kathryn Graddy' Website

Welcome! I am Kathryn Graddy. I am an economist and I hold the Fred and Rita Richman Distinguished Chair in Economics at Brandeis University. My research explores the economics of art and culture, as well as industrial organization. I have a specific interest in the pricing and intellectual property of art and in the price of fish.  Please enjoy exploring my website!
